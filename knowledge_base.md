Vivekananda Institute Of Professional Studies — FAQ Knowledge Base:

Attendance & Exams:
1. A minimum of 75% attendance is required in each subject to be eligible for midsem exams.
2. Students below 75% but above 65% attendance can apply for condonation with a valid medical certificate.
3. Mid-semester exams are held in week 8 of each semester; end-semester exams are held in the last two weeks.
4. Revaluation requests must be submitted within 7 days of result declaration.

Timetable:
5. Classes run Monday to Friday or Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM for different batches.
6. Saturdays-Sundays & Sundays-Mondays are Holidays according to batch.

Hostel:
7. No hostel availability.

Canteen:
8. Canteen is available from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday on prepaid basis.

Library:
9. The library is open 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM from Monday to Saturday.

Fees:
10. Semester fees are due by the 1st of the first month of each new semester.
11. A late fee of ₹100 per day applies after the due date.
